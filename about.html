Our Story

Founded in 2019, Garuda Recruitment Agency was born from a simple vision: to revolutionize the recruitment industry by putting people first.

Named after the mythical bird known for its strength and vision, we embody these qualities in every placement we make. What started as a small boutique agency has grown into a trusted partner for over 250 companies.

Today, we've successfully placed over 8,000 candidates. But we're most proud of the lasting relationships we've built along the way.
